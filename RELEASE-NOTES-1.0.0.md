First public release of Biblo.

**Biblo** reads the PDFs already sitting on your computer, indexes them locally
and lets you question them in plain language. Every answer cites the document
and the page it came from.

### What's in it

- **Answers with sources** — under every answer you get the files it used and
  the passages it quoted, with page numbers; one click opens the PDF at the
  right spot.
- **Specialised assistants** — summarise a volume, compare two texts, find a
  passage, analyse an argument: Biblo picks the one that fits your question,
  or you pick it yourself.
- **The library in three phases** — Read, Inspect, Find connections: kept
  separate, so you can read everything now and go deeper later.
- **Smart search** — describe a topic and get the volumes that actually
  discuss it, ranked by relevance.
- **Citation index** — works, authors and figures cited across your texts.
- **OCR included** — scanned PDFs become searchable too.
- **Interface in 5 languages** (Italian, English, French, Spanish, German),
  light and dark themes, adjustable zoom.

### Installing

Download `Biblo_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe` and run it. No administrator rights
needed. On first launch you'll need a **Cerebras** API key (free).

Windows will show "Unknown publisher" because the installer isn't signed yet:
**More info** → **Run anyway**.
